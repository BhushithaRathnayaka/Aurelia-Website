Our Story

Aurelia opened in 2012 with a single idea: cook slowly, source locally and let every ingredient speak for itself. We work with small farms and fishermen, and change the menu with the seasons.

Behind the amber-lit dining room, Chef Elena Marchetti and her team build each plate by hand.
